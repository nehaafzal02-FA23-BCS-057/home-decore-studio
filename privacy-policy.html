Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Home Decor Studio handles information when you use our website.

When you contact Home Decor Studio or use our website, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide.

  • Messages submitted through our contact form

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information provided by visitors to respond to inquiries, understand project requirements and provide better interior design services.

We may collect basic technical information such as browser type, device type and general website usage information. This helps us improve website performance and provide a better experience.

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality and understand how visitors interact with our pages. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

5. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through our website. However, no method of transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as maps, fonts, images or other external resources. These services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general audiences. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be reflected on this page with the updated policy information.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Home Decor Studio through our Contact Us page.
