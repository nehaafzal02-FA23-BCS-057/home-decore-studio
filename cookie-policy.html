Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are and how Home Decor Studio may use them on our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information and provide a better browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

Home Decor Studio may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality and understand how visitors use our website.

  • To improve website performance
  • To remember basic website preferences
  • To understand visitor interaction with our pages
  • To improve user experience

3. Types of Cookies

Different types of cookies may be used for different purposes.

  • Essential Cookies: These may be necessary for basic website functionality.
  • Performance Cookies: These may help us understand how visitors interact with different pages.
  • Preference Cookies: These may remember certain choices and preferences.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some external services used on our website may place their own cookies or use similar technologies. These services may include maps, analytics, fonts or other website resources.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and cookie practices.

5. Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through their settings. You can choose to block cookies, although some website features may not work as expected.

6. Your Choice

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Continuing to use our website may allow cookies according to your browser configuration.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about our Cookie Policy, please visit our Contact Us page.
