Find Your Perfect Interior Style

Explore our collection of popular interior design styles and discover the aesthetic that best matches your lifestyle, space and personality.

Modern

Clean lines, practical layouts and contemporary furniture create a polished modern environment.

Perfect for clients who prefer clean architecture, neutral colors and functional spaces.

Minimalist

A calm approach focused on simplicity, essential furniture and uncluttered spaces.

Minimalist interiors use fewer elements while focusing strongly on quality, balance and natural light.

Luxury

Premium materials, statement furniture and elegant details create sophisticated interiors.

Luxury design can include marble, premium lighting, custom furniture and rich textures.

Scandinavian

Bright, comfortable and functional interiors inspired by simplicity and natural materials.

This style works beautifully with light colors, wooden textures, simple furniture and cozy details.

Industrial

Raw materials, exposed textures and bold elements create a distinctive urban character.

Brick, metal, concrete, darker tones and open layouts are common features of this style.

Traditional

Classic furniture, warm colors and timeless decorative details create welcoming spaces.

Traditional interiors focus on comfort, symmetry, craftsmanship and classic decorative elements.

Japandi

A balanced combination of Japanese simplicity and Scandinavian warmth.

Japandi emphasizes natural materials, neutral colors, simplicity and peaceful environments.

Rustic

Natural textures, wood and earthy colors create a warm and comfortable atmosphere.

Rustic design is ideal for creating cozy spaces using wood, stone, natural fabrics and earthy tones.

Contemporary

Flexible and evolving design that combines current trends with timeless functionality.

Contemporary interiors can mix modern furniture, elegant textures, artistic elements and practical layouts.
