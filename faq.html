Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our interior design services, consultations, pricing and projects.

1. What services does Home Decor Studio provide?

We provide interior design solutions for living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, bathrooms, offices and complete homes. Our services include design planning, space styling, color selection, furniture planning and interior consultation.

2. Do you provide interior design services across Pakistan?

Yes. Home Decor Studio provides interior design services and consultation for clients in different cities across Pakistan. Online consultations may also be available depending on the project requirements.

3. How much does an interior design project cost?

The cost depends on the size of the space, design requirements, materials, furniture and project scope. Contact us to discuss your requirements and receive a suitable quotation.

4. Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. We offer an initial consultation so that we can understand your design preferences, project requirements and estimated budget before planning the project.

5. How long does an interior design project take?

Project duration depends on the size and complexity of the work. A single-room project may take less time than a complete home project. A final timeline can be discussed during consultation.

6. Can you design a small room?

Absolutely. Small rooms can be designed with smart furniture placement, suitable lighting, storage solutions and appropriate colors to make the space comfortable and functional.

7. Do you provide 3D design ideas?

Yes. Depending on the project, design concepts and visual ideas can be prepared to help clients understand how the proposed space may look before implementation.

8. Can I choose my own colors and furniture?

Yes. Client preferences are an important part of our design process. We can work with your preferred colors, furniture styles and materials while providing professional design suggestions.

9. Do you provide complete home interior design?

Yes. We can plan interior design solutions for multiple rooms, including living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, bathrooms and workspaces, depending on the project requirements.

10. How can I request a quotation?

You can visit our Contact Us page and send your project details. Our team can then review your requirements and discuss the next steps with you.

11. Can I contact Home Decor Studio through WhatsApp?

Yes. Our website provides a WhatsApp contact option for visitors who want to discuss their interior design requirements quickly.

12. Do you provide design ideas for Pakistani homes?

Yes. Our website includes interior design ideas and articles that can help Pakistani homeowners choose suitable colors, furniture, layouts and decoration styles for their homes.
