5 Budget-Friendly Living Room Decor Ideas for 2026

Transforming your living room doesn't have to be expensive. The first step is to create a focal point. Choose one wall and paint it a bold color like navy blue or forest green, or add textured wallpaper.

Focus on soft furnishings, cushion covers, throw blankets and area rugs. Add floor lamps and indoor plants to create a warm and welcoming atmosphere.

AUGUST 1, 2026 | BEDROOM

How to Get a 5-Star Hotel Style Bedroom at Home

The key to a luxurious bedroom is simplicity and comfort. Start with a neutral color palette. Whites, creams and light greys create a calm and relaxing environment.

An upholstered headboard adds instant elegance. Use floor-length curtains, symmetrical nightstands, matching lamps and a large mirror to create a premium hotel-style look.

JULY 25, 2026 | KITCHEN

10 Ways to Make Your Small Kitchen Look Bigger

Small kitchens can feel spacious with the right design tricks. Light colors such as white and light grey reflect light and make the space feel more open.

Use ceiling-height cabinets, open shelves, pull-out drawers and under-cabinet LED lighting. Most importantly, keep countertops clear because clutter makes kitchens feel smaller.

8 Creative Ways to Decorate Your Blank Walls

Empty walls are a blank canvas. A gallery wall is a perfect way to display your personality. Mix different frame sizes and art styles for an interesting visual arrangement.

Floating shelves, mirrors, macrame wall hangings and removable wallpaper can also add personality and texture without making the room feel crowded.
