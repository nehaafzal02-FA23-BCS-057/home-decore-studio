5 Budget-Friendly Living Room Decor Ideas for 2026

Transforming your living room doesn't have to be expensive. The first step is to create a focal point. Choose one wall and paint it a bold color like navy blue or forest green, or add a textured wallpaper. This immediately adds depth and personality to the room.

Next, focus on soft furnishings. New cushion covers and a throw blanket can completely change the look of your sofa. Add a large area rug to define the seating area. For lighting, use a combination of overhead lights and floor lamps to create a warm ambiance. Finally, bring in life with 2-3 indoor plants. With just PKR 25,000, you can give your living room a brand new look.

AUGUST 1, 2026 | BEDROOM

How to Get a 5-Star Hotel Style Bedroom at Home

The key to a luxurious bedroom is simplicity and comfort. Start with a neutral color palette. Whites, creams, and light greys create a calm and serene environment perfect for relaxation. Invest in quality bedding with multiple layers: fitted sheet, flat sheet, duvet, and at least 4 decorative pillows.

An upholstered headboard adds instant elegance. Make sure your curtains are long and reach the floor to make the room feel taller. Keep nightstands symmetrical with matching lamps on both sides. Add a large mirror to reflect light and make the space feel bigger. A subtle room diffuser completes the 5-star hotel experience.

JULY 25, 2026 | KITCHEN

10 Ways to Make Your Small Kitchen Look Bigger

Small kitchens can feel spacious with the right design tricks. First, use light colors. White and light grey cabinets reflect light and make the space feel open. Install cabinets that go all the way to the ceiling to maximize storage and draw the eye up.

Replace some upper cabinets with open shelves to reduce visual weight. Use smart storage solutions like pull-out drawers and corner carousels. Add under-cabinet LED lights for both task lighting and ambiance. The most important rule: keep countertops clear. Clutter makes any kitchen feel small and messy.

JULY 18, 2026 | WALL DECOR

8 Creative Ways to Decorate Your Blank Walls

Empty walls are a blank canvas. A gallery wall is a perfect way to display your personality. Mix different frame sizes and art styles. For a modern look, choose one large statement art piece above your sofa. Mirrors are another great option as they reflect light and make rooms feel larger.

Floating shelves are both decorative and functional. Use them to display books, plants, and decor items. Add texture with a macrame wall hanging or woven basket. For renters, removable wallpaper on an accent wall is a great way to add pattern without commitment.

Top 5 Color Trends for Pakistani Homes in 2026

This year's color trends are all about warmth and comfort. The top palette is Terracotta paired with Cream for an earthy and cozy feel. Second is Sage Green with Beige, which brings a natural and calming vibe to any room.

Third, Navy Blue with Gold accents creates a luxurious and timeless look. Fourth, Dusty Pink with Light Grey offers a soft and modern elegance. Finally, Charcoal Grey with Mustard Yellow makes a bold and sophisticated statement. Remember to use the 60-30-10 rule when applying these colors.

JULY 3, 2026 | DIY IDEAS

Decorate a Room for Under PKR 5000: Complete DIY Guide

You can transform any room on a tight budget. Start by giving old furniture a new life with a coat of paint. A can of chalk paint costs around PKR 800. Next, make your own cushion covers. Buy fabric from the local market for around PKR 1200 and you can make 4-5 covers.

Spend PKR 1500 on decor items from thrift stores or Sunday bazaars. Create your own wall art with a PKR 500 canvas and some acrylic paints. Finally, use the last PKR 1000 to buy 2-3 small indoor plants. DIY projects not only save money but also make your home uniquely yours.
